This is the fourth version of this website. Every version has been a fairly accurate picture of what I cared about that year.

<!-- placeholder: 16:10, v1–v4 screenshots in a 2×2 grid -->
![four screenshots of past versions of this site](../images/this-site-1.png)
<small>clockwise from top left: 2021, 2023, early 2026, now.</small>

- **v1, `2021`.** One HTML file, a typing animation, a list of every language I'd touched. I was in high school and very proud of the animation.
- **v2, `2023`.** React and Framer Motion. Everything faded, slid, or bounced. You could not read it on a phone.
- **v3, `2026`.** Astro, Libre Baskerville, a grid of project cards, and a small drawing of me that blinks when your cursor touches it. The first version I wasn't embarrassed to send to recruiters.
- **v4, now.** This one.

v3 was a portfolio: finished projects with good screenshots. But most of what I do in a year isn't a finished project. It's a term, a trip, a book, an idea that didn't make it. ==I wanted a place where those count too.==

So v4 is an index on the left and writing on the right. Projects are entries like everything else, just with a different tag. The blinking guy stayed. He's earned it.

Rules for this version:
- no animation except the blink
- write the entry before polishing the screenshot
- if a page needs a design change, the design is wrong, not the page
